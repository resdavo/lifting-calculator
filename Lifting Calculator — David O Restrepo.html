From the mid-point between the ankles to the hands. This is the single biggest lever: doubling H roughly halves what you may lift.

Knuckle height (~75 cm) is ideal. Floor-level and overhead picks are penalised.

Worked out automatically as |set-down − pick-up|. Direction is also derived: Up if set-down is higher, Down if lower, Same if equal. At or below 25 cm (about 10 in) the travel multiplier DM stays at 1.00 — no penalty and no bonus.

How far the load is turned away from straight in front of you. 0° = no twist.

Lifts per minute.

This is the total for the whole shift, not per hour. Add together all the minutes in the shift in which at least one lift happens. Count any minute in which at least one lift happens as one minute of lifting — no matter how many lifts happen in that minute, and no matter how long each lift takes in seconds. Do not count minutes with no lift, and do not add up the seconds. Example: 2 lifts in each of 6 consecutive minutes = enter 6. If lifts happen in minute 1, 2 and 3, then not again until minute 11, enter 4, not 11.

Used only for the separate full-shift maximum estimate in the result, which is re-checked in the 2–8 hour duration band before being scaled to the shift. It does not change the recommended weight limit, the Lifting Index, or the maximum lifts in your entered lifting period. Caution: the NIOSH equation is validated for work periods up to 8 hours, so results for 10- and 12-hour shifts should be treated with extra caution.

What is cutting your limit?

Each multiplier starts at 1.00 (ideal) and only goes down. RWL = 23 kg × HM × VM × DM × AM × FM × CM.*
